# Rwanda: internet use and mobile subscriptions, 2010 to 2024

## Internet use

The share of individuals using the internet in Rwanda was 8.0 percent of the population in 2010 and 12.5 percent in 2015. It reached 23.8 percent in 2020, 28.0 percent in 2022, 28.8 percent in 2023, and 31.7 percent in 2024, the latest year available.

The period of fastest growth was 2015 to 2020, when the share nearly doubled. Growth slowed between 2020 and 2023 and picked up again in 2024.

## Mobile subscriptions

Mobile cellular subscriptions per 100 people rose from 34.4 in 2010 to 75.3 in 2015 and 81.2 in 2020. The figure was 80.6 in 2022, 91.5 in 2023, and 93.2 in 2024. Subscriptions per 100 people remained below 100 throughout the period.

## Population

Rwanda's population was 10.3 million in 2010 and 14.3 million in 2024.

## Reading the two indicators together

Mobile subscriptions grew faster than internet use in the early 2010s. From 2020 the two indicators moved more closely together, with internet use at a little under a third of the population in 2024.
