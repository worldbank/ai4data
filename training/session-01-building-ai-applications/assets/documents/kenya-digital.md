# Kenya: internet use and mobile subscriptions, 2010 to 2024

## Internet use

The share of individuals using the internet in Kenya rose from 7.2 percent of the population in 2010 to 16.6 percent in 2015 and 24.7 percent in 2020. Growth continued after 2020: 29.4 percent in 2022, 32.1 percent in 2023, and 35.0 percent in 2024, the latest year available.

Over the full period the share increased by about 28 percentage points. The fastest growth in absolute terms came between 2010 and 2015, when the share more than doubled.

## Mobile subscriptions

Mobile cellular subscriptions per 100 people stood at 60.0 in 2010, 80.1 in 2015, and 117.6 in 2020. The figure was 121.2 in 2022, 120.6 in 2023, and 126.5 in 2024. Values above 100 reflect multiple subscriptions per person and inactive SIM cards, so the indicator measures subscriptions, and no inference about the share of people with a phone follows from it.

## Population

Kenya's population was 41.6 million in 2010 and 56.4 million in 2024.

## Reading the two indicators together

Mobile subscriptions passed one per person around 2020, while internet use remained at about a third of the population in 2024. The gap between the two indicators is a common pattern where mobile coverage precedes affordable data and devices that can use it.
