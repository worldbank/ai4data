# Rwanda: GDP per capita and growth, 2010 to 2024

## GDP per capita

GDP per capita in current US dollars was 587 in 2010, 726 in 2015, and 803 in 2020. It passed 1,000 in 2022 at 1,007, reached 1,061 in 2023, and was 1,060 in 2024. The series is in current dollars, so exchange rates and prices affect it as well as real output.

## Growth

Real GDP growth was 7.3 percent in 2010 and 8.9 percent in 2015. Output contracted by 2.8 percent in 2020. Growth was 9.8 percent in 2022, 8.6 percent in 2023, and 7.2 percent in 2024.

## Reading the two indicators together

Rwanda recorded growth above 7 percent in 10 of the 15 years of the period; 2020 was the only year of contraction. GDP per capita in current dollars rose by about 80 percent between 2010 and 2024 from a lower starting level than Kenya's.
