# Kenya: GDP per capita and growth, 2010 to 2024

## GDP per capita

GDP per capita in current US dollars was 1,092 in 2010, 1,489 in 2015, and 1,928 in 2020. It reached 2,110 in 2022, fell to 1,943 in 2023, and stood at 2,133 in 2024. Because the series is in current dollars, movements reflect exchange rates and prices as well as real output; the 2023 decline coincides with a depreciation of the shilling.

## Growth

Real GDP growth was 8.1 percent in 2010 and 5.0 percent in 2015. Output contracted by 0.3 percent in 2020. Growth was 4.9 percent in 2022, 5.7 percent in 2023, and 4.7 percent in 2024.

## Reading the two indicators together

Kenya's economy grew in every year of the period except 2020. GDP per capita in current dollars roughly doubled between 2010 and 2024, with the exchange rate accounting for part of the year-to-year variation.
