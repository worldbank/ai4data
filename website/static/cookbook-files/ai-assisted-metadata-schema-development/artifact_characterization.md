# Artifact characterization for the teaching collection

Evidence status: this is a teaching restatement of the Data Snapshot study's
scope, prepared for the cookbook. It is not a recovered historical worksheet
or a new empirical result. Source: Dy & Solatorio (2026), working paper,
Sections 3–5.

| Question | Filled teaching statement |
|---|---|
| What is one artifact? | A semantically meaningful analytical table, chart, map, or composite figure treated as an independent information object. |
| Where is its boundary? | The selected snapshot image defines the observed object. Missing captions or notes remain missing; document context is identified separately. |
| What should its metadata support? | Identification, interpretation, cataloging, filtering, and discovery of the artifact and the data it represents. |
| What metadata belongs to the artifact? | Its title, represented indicator, time and geographic context, units, and relevant provenance are candidate descriptive requirements. |
| What is analytical content? | Individual cell values, bar heights, reconstructed series, and other numerical observations are outside this schema-development exercise. |
| What belongs to the parent document? | Document administration such as publication metadata and retrieval URLs remains on its linked document record; it can also supply explicitly identified discovery context. |
| Who decides the final scope? | A domain expert and metadata designer adjudicate concepts and operational fields after discovery. |
| Which diversity is considered? | The study considered three institutional corpora and figures and tables. A new application must also consider its own reporting styles, languages, and analytical contexts. |

The Jordan chart has a title about food-expenditure shares, numbered categories,
percent units, and a World Bank source note. Its proposed geographic value was
inferred from document context, not read from the image. The Afghanistan table
has income categories and a source phrase. The Russian chart has a caption and
an internal heading with different wording. These distinctions motivate
independent discovery; they do not fix a field list in advance.

For another artifact class, replace each filled statement with evidence and a
reason specific to that class. Record the intended users, accepted source
context, excluded contents, reviewer, and version. A useful scope statement
lets two reviewers explain what one record describes and why a proposed value
is inside or outside that boundary.
