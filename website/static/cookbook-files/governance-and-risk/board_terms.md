# AI governance board: terms of reference

**Organization:** National Statistics Office (example)
**Adopted:** 2025-09-15 (decision D001)

## Purpose

The board decides whether an AI component may be used in the production or
dissemination of official statistics, under what controls, and whether it
may continue. It records every decision in the decision log.

## Membership

| Member | Role on the board |
|---|---|
| Director General | Chair; accepts residual risk for High-tier components |
| Head of methodology | Secretary; keeps the register, the risk register, and the decision log |
| Head of dissemination | Member; owns user-facing components and the statement of model use |
| Head of IT | Member; owns providers, hosting, and the cost record |
| Legal adviser | Member; owns licences and terms of use |
| One subject-matter head, rotating | Member; speaks for the production units |

## Decisions the board takes

- Approval of a new component, with its risk tier and the controls its tier
  requires.
- Approval of a change of model, provider, prompt, or data for an existing
  component in the High tier.
- Closure of an incident, after the cause is known and the control that
  prevents it is in place.
- The outcome of each scheduled review: continue, change, or switch off.
- Acceptance of residual risk for High-tier components, by the chair.

## Procedure

1. The component owner submits the register row, the risk rating, and the
   evaluation report at least one week before the meeting.
2. The secretary checks the submission with the register and risk scripts
   and circulates the results.
3. The board decides. A High-tier component needs the chair's acceptance of
   residual risk in the same decision.
4. The secretary records the decision with any follow-up, its owner, and its
   due date.

## Schedule

The board meets every quarter and within five working days of an incident
that reached users.
