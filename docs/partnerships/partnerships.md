# Partnerships

The program works with national statistical organizations, international statistical bodies, and open-source communities on three things: the AI-readiness assessment and its operational companion, the cookbook series, and the tools in the catalogue. A partnership takes one of three forms: an organization runs the assessment and an improvement plan with the program's support, an organization adopts or co-develops a tool and records the installation test, or a standards body or community reviews and reuses the methods.

Partners are listed here as organizations once the agreements are public. Organizations planning an adoption or a joint implementation can write to the Development Data Group at [aifordata@worldbank.org](mailto:aifordata@worldbank.org).
