## Interpretation of the result

- Chapters 1 and 2 first: the rule and the shortlist are a week of
  work and decide where every other chapter spends effort.
- Chapter 6 before any production use: a verified, isolated server is
  the difference between an open model and an open door.
- Chapter 7 is where the decision to go local is made or unmade; the
  arithmetic is short and the staffing question is the real one.
