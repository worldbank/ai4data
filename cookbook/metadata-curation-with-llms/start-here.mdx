## Interpretation of the result

- Chapter 1 first: the rule on confidential records and on curator
  approval takes a day and prevents the costliest mistakes.
- Chapter 2 where the records are in spreadsheets: nothing else can run
  on them until they are in the schema.
- Chapter 5 before scaling chapters 3 and 7: a drafting or translation
  run without a review board and recorded decisions produces text nobody
  checked.
- Chapter 8 is where the investment pays: without recorded decisions,
  every prompt change is a guess.
