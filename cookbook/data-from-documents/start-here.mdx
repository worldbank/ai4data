## Interpretation of the result

- A **Not yet** result in chapter 1 comes first: an inventory is a day of
  work and decides where to spend the rest, including how many pages need
  the OCR of chapter 2.
- Chapters 3 and 4 depend on each other; a team can start with tables from
  the text layer (chapter 4, recipe 4.1) before any layout detection.
- Chapter 5 is the one not to skip: an extracted value that was never
  checked is a transcription, and the chapter's checks use what the
  document already offers.
- Chapter 9 matters once the first fifty documents are done; before that,
  the measurements it asks for do not exist.
