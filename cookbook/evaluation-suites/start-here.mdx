## Interpretation of the result

- Chapters 1 and 2 first: without an inventory and a real question set,
  every measure in the later chapters measures the wrong thing well.
- Chapter 7 is the one that changes behaviour: a suite that gates changes
  is run; a suite that only reports is forgotten.
- Chapter 6 before relying on any grader: an unvalidated grader is a
  second system to be wrong.
