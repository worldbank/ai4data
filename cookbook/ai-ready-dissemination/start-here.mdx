# Self-assessment

Select the statement that best describes the organization today, one per chapter.
The result shows a level for each chapter and the chapters to read first.
Answers are stored in this browser only.

For a fuller assessment, with indicators and a country pathway, see the
program's [AI-readiness assessment framework](/ai-readiness-assessment).
The [From assessment to action](/ai-readiness-in-practice) page maps each
of its dimensions to the chapters and tools here.

<SelfAssessment />

## Interpretation of the result

- A **Not yet** or **Foundational** result in chapters 1 to 3 comes before
  anything else. The later chapters depend on complete metadata, open
  formats, and documented meaning.
- Chapters 6 (Evaluate) and 8 (Govern) can start early at a small scale: a
  question set of 30 items and a one-page policy take a day each and make
  every later step measurable and safe.
- Chapter 4 (Ask) at the AI-native level, a conversational interface, is
  worth starting only when chapters 1, 2, 5, and 6 are at AI-ready.
