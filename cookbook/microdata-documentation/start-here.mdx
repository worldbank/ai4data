## Interpretation of the result

- A **Not yet** or **Foundational** result in chapters 1 to 3 comes first:
  study-level and variable-level documentation are what every later chapter
  reads, and chapter 2 is where that documentation comes from when the
  files exist and the records do not.
- Chapter 8 can start at any time; the access policy is a one-day task that
  makes the rest safe to publish.
- Chapters 4 to 6 give the largest gains for users once chapter 3 is at
  AI-ready, because concept links, thematic groups, and crosswalks turn a
  long dictionary into something searchable and comparable.
