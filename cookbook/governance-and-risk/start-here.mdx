chapter. The result shows a level for each chapter and the chapters to read
first. Answers are stored in this browser only.

## Interpretation of the result

- A **Not yet** or **Foundational** result in chapters 1 and 2 comes before
  anything else. Every other instrument refers to the policy and the
  register.
- Chapters 3 and 4 can be done in the same week as the register, because
  the ratings and the approvals are what the register's rows wait for.
- Chapters 5 and 6 depend on the legal adviser's time; the licence matrix
  is worth starting early because it is the evidence for a whole
  assessment dimension.
- Chapter 7 is worth doing for one release as soon as a component in the
  Medium or High tier has a suite, and chapter 8 is the first task of each
  quarter after that.
