## Interpretation of the result

- Chapter 1 first: without a canonical names table, detection and
  harmonization measure noise.
- Chapters 2 and 3 are the cheapest evidence: logging and DOIs take days
  and the counts arrive every month after that.
- Chapters 4 to 6 can start small; a few sources and a labelled sample of
  a hundred sentences give the first real numbers.
- Chapter 9 is where the value is realized; a report that changes nothing is
  a cost.
