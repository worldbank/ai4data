## Interpretation of the result

- Chapter 1 first: the map and its rules are a day of work and decide
  where every other chapter applies.
- Chapter 3 is where most organizations start in practice; the threshold
  rule and the re-coded sample are what make it a production method.
- Chapter 7 is the one that keeps the statistics official; a release with
  model assistance and no statement is a release with an undocumented
  method.
