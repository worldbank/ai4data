# Standards used in this guide

The recipes follow the AI-readiness assessment framework for national
statistical organizations and the planning logic it sets out. Where the
program's resources serve a step, the guide uses them.

## Assessment framework

| Standard | Use | In this guide |
|---|---|---|
| [AI-readiness assessment framework](/ai-readiness-assessment) | The two pillars, twelve dimensions, sixty-nine questions, four levels, and the scoring rule the profile reproduces | Chapters [1](./profile.mdx), [2](./targets.mdx), and [7](./reassess.mdx) |
| The framework's four-step logic (prioritize, diagnose, plan, monitor) | The order of the chapters: profile and gaps, reading the questions, targets and sequence and pathway, evidence and re-assessment | All chapters |
| The framework's pathway for early-stage organizations | The four foundations the sequence check verifies | Chapter [3](./sequence.mdx) |
| [Committee for the Coordination of Statistical Activities](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/ccsa/) | The recommendations on AI-ready official statistics that the framework's questions map to | Chapter 1 |

## Planning and governance

| Standard | Use | In this guide |
|---|---|---|
| [ISO/IEC 42001](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) | Planning, objectives, and management review of an AI management system, which the targets, the pathway, and the quarterly check implement | Chapters 2, [5](./pathway.mdx), and [6](./evidence.mdx) |
| [UN National Quality Assurance Frameworks manual](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/dataquality/) | The quality management the pathway's quality dimension serves | Chapter 2 |

## Program methods and tools

| Resource | Use | In this guide |
|---|---|---|
| [Operational companion](/ai-readiness-in-practice) | Program resources, recipes, progression between levels, and the investment view per dimension | Chapters 2, 3, and [4](./effort.mdx) |
| [Governance cookbook](/cookbook/governance-and-risk/) | The board, the decision log, and the governance review that produce the pathway's approvals and part of its evidence pack | Chapters 5, 6, and 7 |
| [A modern statistical office](/modern-nso) | The destination at three maturity levels, for setting targets | Chapter 2 |
