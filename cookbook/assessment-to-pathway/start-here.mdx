## Interpretation of the result

- A **Not yet** or **Foundational** result in chapters 1 and 2 comes before
  anything else: the profile and the targets are what every later step
  computes from.
- Chapters 3 and 4 take an hour once the tables exist, and chapter 5 is
  the day that turns them into a decision.
- Chapter 6 is worth starting in the first month of the pathway, because
  the quarterly check is where a slipping wave is seen in time.
- Chapter 7 is read at the start of the cycle, so that the re-assessment
  is scheduled and its version fixed before the work begins.
