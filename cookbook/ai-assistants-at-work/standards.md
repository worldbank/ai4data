# Standards used in this guide

The recipes follow the organization's AI-use policy and the principles
of official statistics it restates. Where the program's methods serve a
step, the guide uses them.

## Principles and policy

| Standard | Use | In this guide |
|---|---|---|
| [UN Fundamental Principles of Official Statistics](https://unstats.un.org/fpos/) | Confidentiality of individual data and the organization's responsibility for what it publishes, which the tasks that stay with the person and the tiers follow from | Chapters [1](./tasks.mdx) and [2](./inputs.mdx) |
| [AI-use policy and task boundary](/cookbook/governance-and-risk/position) | The permitted and prohibited uses, the three data tiers, and the task boundary the card is the everyday part of | Chapters 1, 2, and [5](./record.mdx) |
| [ISO/IEC 42001](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) | Awareness and competence of staff, and operational control of AI use, which the card, the session, and the log implement | Chapters 1, 5, and [6](./unit.mdx) |
| [OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-for-large-language-model-applications/) | Sensitive information disclosure and over-reliance, the two everyday risks the tiers and the checks address | Chapters 2 and [4](./checking.mdx) |

## Disclosure and identifiers

## Program methods and tools

| Resource | Use | In this guide |
|---|---|---|
| [Proof-Carrying Numbers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06902) | Verified numbers in generated text, of which the number check is the everyday form | Chapters [3](./asking.mdx) and 4 |
| [Language Models in Statistical Production](/cookbook/language-models-in-production/commentary) | The number and claim check of a commentary draft, which the summary prompt and the check follow | Chapters 3 and 4 |
| [Small and Open Models](/cookbook/small-and-open-models/serving) | The local model server behind the local assistant | Chapter 2 |
| [Component register](/cookbook/governance-and-risk/register) | The row everyday assistants occupy, with the card as review and the log as measure | Chapter 5 |
| [AI-readiness assessment framework](/ai-readiness-assessment) | The questions on AI literacy, productivity tools, and change management the sessions and the log are evidence for | Chapter 6 |
