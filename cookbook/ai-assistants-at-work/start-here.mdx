## Interpretation of the result

- A **Not yet** result in chapters 1 and 2 comes before anything else:
  the card and the tiers are what every other habit refers to.
- Chapters 3 and 4 are taught together in the unit session of chapter 6,
  and the session is the fastest way from Not yet to Foundational in
  four chapters at once.
- Chapter 5 is where the practice becomes governance; its first summary
  a month after the session is the first evidence the board sees.
