## Interpretation of the result

- Chapters 1, 5, and 6 come first: scope, access, and safety are
  decisions, and they are cheap compared with their cost when skipped.
- Chapter 2 depends on the API and catalog described in the dissemination
  cookbook; a server over an undocumented API inherits every gap, and
  chapter 3 is where the gaps the tools cannot close are stated.
- Chapter 7 is the gate for publication: a server without an evaluation
  suite cannot be changed safely.
- Chapter 8 is the one most often forgotten; a server nobody can configure
  has no users.
